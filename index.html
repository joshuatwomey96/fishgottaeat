Fish Gotta Eat

A snack-sized aquarium adventure by Dryfitted

Coming soon to Android

Feed your fish, time perfect taps and grow a lively pixel aquarium. Every bite pays. Spend it on upgrades, tank buddies, new fish and a Kelp Tree that keeps growing, then outgrow your tank and move on: from a little Fishbowl, through the Pond and the Reef, out to the open Ocean and down into the glowing Abyss.

  • Six tanks, each with its own fish, critters and surprises
  • Upgrades, buddies, hatching and a Kelp Tree full of fun moves
  • Plays offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
