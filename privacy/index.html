Fish Gotta Eat

Privacy policy

Developer: Dryfitted
Updated: 19 October 2026

Fish Gotta Eat is an offline game. The game does not collect or transmit personal information, use analytics or advertising services, or require an account. It does not include ads or in-app purchases.

Game progress, fish names and preferences are stored locally on your device. They stay there until you reset the game or delete its app data. Uninstalling the Android app removes its local saves. Android app backup is disabled; there is currently no cloud save service.

Google Play handles the purchase and may process information under its own privacy policy. The game does not receive your payment details.

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address and bug details, to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information in a bug report.

Public support contact: fishgottaeatdev@gmail.com
